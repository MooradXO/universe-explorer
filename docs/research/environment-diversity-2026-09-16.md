# Planet and environment diversity — design research

The requirement was a recognizable environment for every planet, not a choice among three limited presets. Variation should include surface structure, atmosphere, rings, stones, local objects, lighting, anomalies and spatial composition. A recoloured rock pile or repeated Plexus effect was insufficient.

Official developer materials were used as references for coherent regional themes, memorable silhouettes, contrasting dense/open space and layered composition. Ideas informed original implementation; reference assets/compositions were not copied.

The design separates deterministic identity, authored structural recipes, parameters, artistic palettes and runtime budgets. Known Solar System positions remain fixed; generated worlds are explicitly fictional. Distinctive Earth red/blue glow can remain a local identity without being repeated everywhere.

The subsequent implementation added structural libraries, verified many worlds in the actual game and eventually exposed those libraries through the composite workshop. New mechanics are separate from visual generation.
